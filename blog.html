Dubai Real Estate Market Outlook 2026: What Investors Need to Know

Dubai's property market is projected to maintain strong momentum into 2026, with residential prices expected to rise by 5-7% annually. Key growth drivers include the upcoming World Expo legacy developments, new visa reforms attracting global talent, and continued infrastructure investments in areas like Dubai Creek Harbour and Mohammed bin Rashid City. International buyers, particularly from India, UK, and Russia, are capitalizing on competitive mortgage rates and the city's zero income tax advantage. The off-plan market remains robust with over 90,000 units in the pipeline, while established communities like Dubai Marina and Palm Jumeirah continue to command premium valuations.
