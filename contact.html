Got Questions? We've Got Answers

Everything you need to know about buying property in Dubai, explained simply

Absolutely! As a foreigner, you can own property outright in Dubai's freehold areas. This means it's 100% yours – you can sell it, rent it out, or pass it on to your family. We help international buyers like you find the perfect property and handle all the paperwork.

Great news – you don't need to pay everything upfront! Most developers offer flexible payment plans. For example, you might pay 20% upfront and the rest over time (that's an 80/20 plan). Some even let you continue payments after you move in. We'll help you find a plan that fits your budget.

Yes! Dubai properties typically earn you 5-8% rental income each year. Think of it this way: if you buy a property for AED 1 million, you could earn AED 50,000-80,000 annually in rent. Plus, your property value often increases over time. It's a win-win!

Beyond the property price, budget for these one-time costs: 4% government transfer fee, around AED 2,000-4,000 for registration, and typically 2% for agent fees. If you're getting a mortgage, there'll be some bank fees too. We'll give you the exact breakdown for your property upfront – no surprises!

Yes! Buy a property worth AED 750,000+ and get a 2-year residence visa. Invest AED 2 million+ and get 5 years. Invest AED 5 million+ and you'll qualify for the prestigious 10-year Golden Visa. Your spouse and children can join you too!

Super fast! For ready properties, you can own it in just 1-2 weeks. We'll help you choose, make an offer, transfer the deed, and get the keys. If you're buying off-plan (under construction), you'll pay in installments as they build, then get the keys when it's ready.

Freehold = it's yours forever! You own it completely and can do whatever you want with it. Leasehold = you own it for a set time (usually 99 years), then it goes back to the landowner. Good news: most properties we work with are freehold, so you own them outright!

Absolutely! UAE banks love lending to expats. If you live here, banks can finance up to 75% of the property price. If you're buying from abroad, expect 50-60% financing. We'll connect you with the best mortgage advisors who'll get you great rates.

No property taxes – that's one of Dubai's best perks! You'll only pay a yearly service charge for things like building maintenance, gym, pool, and security. This usually costs AED 5-25 per square foot annually. For a 1,000 sq ft apartment, that's around AED 5,000-25,000 per year.

That's exactly what we're here for! We'll find great tenants, handle the paperwork, collect rent, fix any issues, and send you monthly updates. You just sit back and watch the money come in. It's truly hassle-free property ownership.
